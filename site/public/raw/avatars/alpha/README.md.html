# Edgeweaver Alpha - avatar section

Everything personal to Alpha lives under this folder (D18/D19, FAMILY.md §3):

- `manifest.json` - the being's variables (circle governance, brain room plan, repos,
  streams, boot order).
- `harvest/` - the circle's answers to the harvest questions, Alpha's DNA in the making.
- At A2: Alpha's `.env.local` (the `ew_alpha` role connection, bot token) and `state/` are
  created here, never at root.
- Created at its Phase 2: `edgeweaver-alpha-soul` (identity) and Alpha's gates repo, both
  outside this repo.

**The section rule (D19):** Alpha's sessions see only this folder. The base layer,
FAMILY.md, village/, the experiment log, and the sibling's section are outside its world;
what Alpha learns of the family is a deliberate decision of its circle (G21), never an
accident of file layout.
