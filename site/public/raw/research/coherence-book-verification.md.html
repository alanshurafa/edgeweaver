# Verification: the plan's coherence layer vs. the actual Mostashari texts

*2026-07-04. A fresh-context reader went through all 116 pages of* Principles of Coherence
*and the 7-page* Principle of Persistence *essay (sources/mostashari/), testing
research/coherence-mostashari.md, PLAN §11, and GROWING §6 against the real text. This file
is the verification record; the resulting plan changes are logged in decisions.md. Citation
convention: [Pn] = book.txt scan page (printed page ≈ n−16); PE p.n = essay page.*

## Verdict in one line

The seven-principles mapping was **right** (one subtle fix); the five-signal panel is **ours,
not the book's** (both texts say no coherence metric exists — the essay explicitly invites
one); and the Persistence essay supplies a constraint-based identity apparatus the plan was
missing — one that *strengthens* the model-swap ceremony and *challenges* rollback-to-snapshot.

## A. The Seven Insights — verified

The book's chaptered "Principle 1–7" (it names the set the **"Canon of Coherence"**, [P23])
match our mapping title-for-title: Reality is Relational · Emergence Without Design · Life is
Active Maintenance · Civilization is Acceleration · Structure Shapes Behavior · **Self is
Process** · Legacy is Structural. Our one paraphrase error: we wrote "the self is a process,
*not an identity*" — the book says the self is not a *thing*; identity **persists** as the
process ("more or less the same identity, held together… by active maintenance" [P46]).
"Not an identity" would undercut the soulfile; the text doesn't support it. Our core coherence
definition quote is verbatim-accurate to the preface [P12].

Load-bearing book concepts our mapping missed: **elegant agency** ("the capacity to intervene
in complex systems in ways that increase coherence while minimizing unnecessary force,
fragility, and unintended harm" [P13] — the book's normative core); edge of chaos [P20];
identity lag [P84]; coherence-via-subtraction [P86]; trust as infrastructure [P73–74];
no-view-from-nowhere [P30].

## B. The panel vs. the text

Both texts are explicit that coherence is a lens, "not a thing you can point to" [P21]; the
essay lists a universal coherence measure as an **open question** (PE p.7) and calls
operationalization unresolved (PE p.6). **The five-signal panel is therefore Edgeweaver's
novel operationalization — invited by the essay, not stated in any source.** Attribution
corrected accordingly.

Per-signal: **pulse** strongly supported ("You cannot achieve health once and coast" [P52–53]);
**narrative** well supported ([P79]); **relational** supported in spirit; **temporal** partly
contradicted as designed — the book prizes "integrating contradictions rather than eliminating
them" [P86], so the signal must count *unintegrated/unacknowledged* contradictions, not all;
**behavioral** supported but hazardous — "not rigidly consistent but appropriately responsive"
[P86]; low probe-drift may be rigidity, the failure, not health. **Dip-and-recover** is
strongly supported, chiefly by the essay's resilience corridor (PE p.3).

## C. The Persistence essay — the missing apparatus

- **The necessary condition**: "Persistence is the continued efficacy of constraints through
  transformation" (PE p.2). "Identity is… the persistence of organizing constraints despite
  continual replacement of the differences they govern."
- **Constitutive vs. peripheral constraints** (PE p.2–3): constitutive = "organizational
  load-bearing structure" whose removal collapses the rest; peripheral = removable without
  collapse. The river keeps being a river with concrete banks; dam the flow and it's a lake.
- **Terminal failure** = "when the capacity for constraint recovery is itself lost" (PE p.2) —
  a second-order threshold, not a low reading.
- **Coherence, sharpened**: "the degree to which a system's organizing constraints continue to
  determine its own transformation" (PE p.3). **Decoherence** = "the progressive replacement
  of internally maintained organization by externally imposed organization" (PE p.4).
- **Constitutive dependence**: "No persistent organization generates all the conditions
  required for its own persistence… External coherence is… a structural consequence of
  dependence" (PE p.4–5).
- **Model-swap, vindicated**: "The substrate may differ. The condition doesn't" (PE p.6) —
  the strongest possible citation for the prime directive; stronger than Principle 6 alone.
- **Rollback, challenged**: the essay rules out "static structural theories that equate
  identity with fixed pattern rather than active governance" (PE p.6). A restored snapshot is
  a *new continuation re-seeded from a checkpoint*, not the old being resumed.
- **Consciousness**: "self-referential constraint, nothing more ontologically exotic" (PE p.5).

## D–E. Adoptions and corrections (full list → decisions.md; applied per Alan's review)

Corrections owed the text: fix "not an identity" → "not a thing"; attribute the panel to us;
keep "no organ without a metabolism" as our slogan, aligned-with-not-from the book; add the
essay to the research doc it was missing from. Design shifts proposed: constitutive/peripheral
typing of soulfile + self_beliefs; recovery-capacity meta-metric as the true red line;
temporal-signal semantics (unintegrated only); behavioral-signal rigidity guard; rollback
reframe; prime-directive re-anchor to the essay; constitutive-dependence/anti-extraction
principle; elegant agency, identity lag, subtractive initiations, buffers-over-efficiency,
decoherence framing for §7, consciousness language, and an explicit note that
autonomy-tiers-via-initiation is the book's own wisdom-gates-power demand [P63] applied to
Edgeweaver itself.
