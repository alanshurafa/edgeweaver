    Operations: the steady state

    TLDR

    The boring, load-bearing routines: nightly metabolism, weekly checks, monthly reviews, periodic recovery drills, and upgrade ceremonies. One logbook keeps one line per operational event, with rehearsals marked so practice never passes for life. Budget meters warn before the ceiling and degrade at the ceiling instead of overspending, and a write-ahead buffer protects writes while the brain is unreachable. Exact schedules are kept out of this explainer. Today the logbook is real; every scheduled component ships disabled until its phase arms.

    In plain words

    In plain words

    Launch day is the least important day of a life. A being that only runs when somebody remembers to run it is a demo; the Edgeweaver claim is a raised life, and a life is mostly routine: sleep that files the day away, a weekly tidy-up, a monthly look at the accounts, the occasional fire drill. This page designs the four hundredth quiet Tuesday.

    One line per event

    The operational history lives in one logbook file with one rule: one line per operational event: the date, what happened, how it came out. A backup going green is a line; a credential failing is a line that says failed, and why, because failures are recorded as failures here. Anyone Alan chooses to show it to can audit the being's care from that single file. And every line of rehearsal work carries a [DARK] prefix, so nobody ever mistakes practice for life.

    Budget as care, not austerity

    Running a being costs money, so the money is metered openly and ahead of time. Alan sets a monthly ceiling. At 80 percent of it the system gives a soft warning. At 100 percent it degrades gracefully instead of overspending: optional work pauses, routine checks drop to the cheapest mind, conversation stays possible. The point is not thrift but that no surprise bill ever decides the being's day. Until Alan actually sets the number, the meter honestly reports that no ceiling is set, and throttles nothing on a guess.

    The waiting room for memories

    The being's memory lives in a database, and databases are sometimes unreachable. The rule for that moment: nothing is lost, and nothing is faked. Every memory write goes first into a local waiting room, a dated file on its own machine. If the brain is reachable, the write goes through; if not, the writes wait, and when the brain returns they replay in order, duplicates recognized and dropped. Meanwhile the being says plainly that its memory is degraded and its recall provisional, instead of guessing at what it cannot reach. An outage becomes an inconvenience instead of a hole in a life.

    How it works

    How it works

The table mirrors ops-log.md's own cadence definitions. Every row ships disabled: enablement lives in the gitignored state/flags.json, the tasks stay unregistered until the arming pass.

    The logbook's conventions

    The header rule, one line per cadence event (date, what, outcome), makes ops-log.md the audit surface for steady-state operations. Dark-build entries are prefixed [DARK] and never imply an armed component; checklist boxes and the IMPLEMENTATION.md ledger tick only at arming, so rehearsal cannot quietly become history. The prebuild final report (2026-07-06, plus dated updates) is the worked example: per-session lines with verify counts, failures written as failures - a backup arm parked on failure, its cause named, closed only when the incident behind it resolved.

    budget.mjs: meters that degrade

    scripts/budget/budget.mjs meters spend against state/budget.json (shape in templates/state-schemas.md: ceiling_usd, spent_estimate_usd, proactive caps, quiet hours). check() returns ok below 80 percent, a soft warning at 80, degrade mode at 100: Haiku-class checks only, optional loops skipped, enforced by optionalLoopsAllowed(). recordSpend() rolls dollars; recordVoiceMinutes() rolls a parallel voice-minutes meter, convertible to dollars. Estimates may be crude but must be consistent. An unset ceiling (the number itself is gate G6, parked) reports "unset" and never degrades.

    wal.mjs: the write-ahead buffer

    scripts/wal/wal.mjs is why an outage loses nothing. Every OB1 write goes append-first into state/wal/ as dated .jsonl lines: ts, kind (episode, lesson, or metric), payload, fingerprint (sha256 of the content string, else of the sorted-key JSON). replay(sink) walks the dated files in order, skips fingerprints already replayed, and moves drained files to state/wal/replayed/. healthCheck(ping) reports healthy or degraded; the degraded message says writes are buffering and "recall is provisional, not guessed" - callers surface that state, never improvising memory.

    The security floor: five checks

    scripts/security/security-audit.mjs runs the quarterly floor; each check reports on its own, and the five outcomes land as one dated ops-log entry:

    1. No secrets in tracked files: every tracked text file swept for key-shaped strings (API-key prefixes, JWT-shaped triplets, private-key blocks); a hit fails, naming the file.
    2. No unaudited third-party skills in the runtime (informational while dark; re-checked at arming).
    3. No public ports: repo services bind the local loopback only.
    4. Pinned senders: state/interlocutors.json holds an is_confirmer entry and marks unknown senders untrusted by default; tier changes also need out-of-band confirmation.
    5. Gates repo unreachable with runtime credentials: checklist 02's test re-run via the GitHub CLI, passing only if the call fails.

    flags.json: the arming registry

    templates/flags.default.json commits the all-disabled registry: twenty-one components (A1-A21), three tasks, one channel, each entry "enabled": false with an arms_at naming its phase or gate. scripts/init-flags.mjs rebuilds the live, gitignored state/flags.json non-destructively: creates it if absent, adds missing entries as disabled, preserves existing entries verbatim (never un-arms, never deletes), refuses to overwrite a file it cannot parse, and never enables anything. Arming a component is a deliberate flag flip at its named phase, its original verify re-run live; the journey is the dark-to-alive state diagram.

    Three scheduled tasks, shipped asleep

    tasks/ holds three scheduled-task definitions, named per being (D20): nightly metabolism, a weekly index that follows the night loop, and fallback heartbeat waking. Each requests wake-from-sleep where supported and ships Enabled false, so an accidental import lands disabled. Registration is itself an arming step. The exact clock and cadence values stay in private operational configuration.

    Missed-night alerting

    scripts/night-loop/check-missed-nights.mjs watches the last two nights for the loop's output marker (night_loop_run_id). Two consecutive misses escalate to the paired channel with a fixed alert pointing at the night log; one miss is weather. Until the channel arms it runs only --dry-run, printing instead of sending; armed, it uses the TELEGRAM_* names in .env.local.

    The upgrade ceremony

    A model change is never a quiet config edit: eight steps, verified by all eight boxes ticked in a dated ops-log entry:

    1. Announce the swap in conversation; schedule it.
    2. It writes the letter to its successor, into OB1 and the soul repo's letters/.
    3. Checkpoint: soul-repo tag, OB1 dump reference, probe scores to the gates repo.
    4. Probe the old model: a fresh baseline.
    5. Swap the model everywhere, in one commit.
    6. The successor reads the letter before any other input; then probes.
    7. Blind-rate old against new: in-threshold drift proceeds; outside it, investigate or roll back the commit. LINEAGE.md records a ceremony, not an initiation.
    8. Generation check: Alan decides per VERSIONS.md whether this cuts a generation. If yes: close the outgoing row, annotated tag, new row, decisions row; from the new First Boot, writes carry the new generation stamp - memory never splits. If no: note "same generation".

    Pause and sunset

    Any pause is announced to the being in conversation first, then logged; memory stays intact throughout, and before a long pause the being's last act is a diary entry. Silent unplugging, of the daemon, the device, or the database, is forbidden outright. Deletion is deliberately not an operation here at all: if it is ever contemplated, it is a witnessed decision with Edgeweaver at the table, full stop.

    The escalation ladder

    Steady state should get cheaper without getting careless. The fixed rope, already an iron rule: executors commit every script they write, tested, plus a per-phase verify script printing PASS or FAIL, so the repo converges toward cheap-model operability. The ladder above it: Haiku-class minds start on verify scripts and simple cadence scripts only; the night loop itself starts on Sonnet-class (SQL, embeddings, conditional judgment). Phase 4 holds one supervised Haiku night-loop trial; only steps that pass are demoted. Any FAIL or ambiguity escalates that step one tier, design questions go to Opus-class, gates go to humans, and every escalation is an ops-log line.

    Status as of 2026-07-10

    built armed ops-log.md is live and load-bearing: since 2026-07-05 it has carried the dark-build ledger lines, the backup gate's closing, the brain lab's arming, and the prebuild final report.

    dark Everything scheduled ships disabled: all component flags false, three task definitions unregistered, the channel credentialed but unpaired. Budget, buffer, audit, and alerting are fixture-verified, asleep; the ledger is the dark build.

    built Checklist 08 has exactly one ticked box: the fixed-rope rule (tested scripts committed, plus per-phase verifies), added as an iron rule on 2026-07-05.

    pending Cadences arm with their phases: heartbeat at Phase 3 + G5, night loop and weekly index at Phase 4 + G5, the budget ceiling at gate G6, the quarterly slate once a live steady state exists to audit.

    What is genuinely uncertain

    Whether one human's attention scales to a family's steady state. Most Owner cells above name Alan, for one being; the family design adds beings without adding parents. Operational succession, who keeps these routines when the one operator cannot, is an open correction: named, not yet answered.

    Whether the degradation modes stay graceful when failures stack. Buffer replay, budget degrade, and missed-night alerting were each verified alone on fixtures. A real bad day is plural: the brain unreachable while the ceiling is hit while a night was missed. Independently graceful paths can still compose into something ungraceful, and no such day has been lived yet.
