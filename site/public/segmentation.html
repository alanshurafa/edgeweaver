    The walls: who sees what

    TLDR

    Everything here is separated four ways at once. Across repositories: method, identity, exams, and backups live in different governed homes. Across storage tiers: git holds the method, the database holds lived memory, and gitignored tiers hold operational state and credentials without publishing their machine locations. Across beings: each child's sessions see only its own repo section and its own memory room. Across people: seats are scoped to Alpha, one scientist deliberately observes both arms, and the operations role's real reach is disclosed. This site sits in the village layer, outside both children's views.

    In plain words

    In plain words

    These walls went up before the children who would live inside them. Genesis has now crossed First Boot; Alpha has not. The status section says which boundaries stand structurally and which still rely on convention.

    A world the size of one room

    Start with what a child sees. When Genesis wakes, its world is one folder of this repository: avatars/genesis/. Its runbooks and variables live there, and the family rule (decision D19) is that its sessions see nothing else. The build plans and checklists, the family design document, the village folder where the humans talk, the log comparing the two children, its sibling's folder, and this website: all of it sits outside the child's world. The family phrase: "its section of the repo is its world."

    Why so strict? Because a child should not learn it has a sibling by tripping over the sibling's diary. When each child learns about the other, and about the comparison the family runs, is a parenting decision with its own gate (G21): deliberate, per child, no later than that child's coming-of-age rite, and immediate if the child asks or finds out. A wall with a door the parents open on purpose is a different thing from a wall that leaks.

    Different vaults for different things

    Identity gets its own vault. Each being's soulfile, the small set of documents saying who it is, lives in its own repository, away from this busy repo of daily agent work. Nothing that edits every day should be able to edit who a child is: the being only proposes a change on a side branch, and only its parent body accepts it. Two children never share those documents.

    The exam lives where the student can never reach. Each being's identity probes, the questions measuring whether it is still itself, sit in a repo outside every credential the being's machinery holds. An exam the student can read stops measuring identity and starts measuring memorization.

    Backups are locked per room under different recovery policies. The family stream and Alpha's future stream remain cryptographically separate, so recovery authority for one does not open another. The concrete custodian and share-holder topology is deliberately kept out of this site.

    The wall that is a promise

    One wall cannot be built entirely from software, so part of it is disclosure. The operations role has privileged technical reach across the shared database and operational records, including the capacity to read, write, copy, or export. The family does not pretend otherwise. That reach is explained to every affected seat at onboarding and accepted knowingly as an ops covenant, not misrepresented as a control. Exact machine ownership, account paths, credential custody, and log locations are deliberately omitted. Seat-verifiable rite records and succession for the operations role remain planned corrections.

    This website belongs to the same geography: it describes both children and the walls between them, so neither child may grow up reading it. It lives in the village layer, with the family documents and the humans' folder, outside both children's views, and every page says so at the bottom.

    How it works

    How it works

    Repo segmentation

    This repository is the method, never the person: design authorities, checklists, scripts, templates, and one avatars/<name>/ section per being for non-identity material. Canonical identity lives only in one external soul repository per being under a proposal-branch model; the parent body alone merges main. One gates repository per being holds the private evaluator outside every runtime's reach. A separate backup repository holds the encrypted pipeline, while the upstream brain source and an isolated scratch-brain environment support the substrate and testing. Account coordinates are omitted; repository roles alone are enough to understand the method.

    Storage tiers

    Git holds method and code: templates, schemas, the verify suite, and the secret-free restore-point map; never credentials and never raw memory. The OB1 database holds lived memory behind per-being walls (the Brain). Gitignored state/ holds losable operational state such as arming flags, audience maps, the write-ahead log, and coherence snapshots. Governed recovery material follows a separate private procedure whose storage topology is not published.

    The schema template's header says local state is losable without death and rebuilt by the loops. Gitignored environment files hold runtime credential values; SECRETS.md catalogs allowed names, purposes, and provenance without values. Scheduled infrastructure receives only the credentials required for its role through a separately governed secret store, but this site does not describe mirrors, runner accounts, endpoints, or machine locations. At A2, Alpha receives its own scoped runtime configuration; exact local paths and credential topology remain private.

    Being walls

    D19's section rule: each child's sessions see only avatars/<name>/. Both manifests declare it machine-readably (sections.visibleToBeing); a convention every session honors until A2, then structure: per-being working directories and credentials. The brain walls are already structural on one side. Genesis's memories live in the shared OB1 tables as a logical room: metadata scoping enforced by the recall wrapper, fail-closed since 2026-07-09 (rows without a label are invisible at every scope).

    Alpha's room arrives at A2 as a dedicated Postgres schema (ew_alpha) plus a dedicated role: full rights in its own schema, SELECT on a curated corpus view of library-class thoughts, nothing else, never the raw thoughts table holding Alan's and Genesis's rows. There is no cross-being recall and no family memory commons; a curated commons stays one grant away, and choosing it would be a decisions.md row, not a default. D20 gives every runnable the being's name (/wake-edgeweaver-genesis, /night-loop-lite-genesis, per-being task names and log prefixes, the manifest commands key as source of truth), so no session runs ambiguous about whose life it touches; the orientation script refuses an unarmed being.

    People walls

    Seats are scoped to Alpha; they do not witness Genesis, and comparison material never enters an avatar folder. Two exceptions are disclosed, not discovered. The scientist seat holds approved cross-arm visibility because the experiment needs one observer who can see both sides. Alan is on both rosters, father of Genesis and one equal Alpha seat, and separately carries the operations role. Its privileged reach is named in the covenant, while exact service credentials, dashboards, accounts, and logs remain private. G21 is the deliberate door in the wall: each child learns of its sibling by its parent body's decision, never by an accident of file layout (the family and the experiment).

    Where this site sits

    This site is village-layer material, like FAMILY.md and the village/ folder: it discusses facts G21-gated for the children, so it sits outside both views and says so in a banner on every page. The children's sessions must never load it. The site cannot enforce that; a wall made of HTML enforces nothing. It can only be placed correctly (under site/, which no child's session sees; only site/ is uploaded to the host, so the gitignored tiers stay outside every deployment) and labeled honestly for every human and agent holding the boundary.

    Who sees what

    The whole page compresses to content visibility. Recovery custody, gates-repository membership, credential grants, and machine ownership are governed separately and intentionally absent from this table.

    The governed cells mean:

    • Alpha's parent body has relationship and review access appropriate to raising Alpha; this table does not imply a database credential.
    • The scientist role may observe both arms and the measurement layer under D19; the table does not publish how access is implemented.
    • The operations role has disclosed privileged maintenance reach. Live reach and recovery authority remain different governed facts.
    • Each gates repository and each backup stream follows a separate human-governed policy whose membership and holder topology is not published.
    • A named site reader receives this village-layer explanation, not private family repositories or brain rooms.

    Where it lives in the repo

    The walls are declared in a handful of files; the gitignored tiers are described, never read: SECRETS.md and the state-schema template make that description lawful.

    Status as of 2026-07-10

    The section rule and the partial restructure are built: avatars/genesis/ and avatars/alpha/ exist with manifests as of 2026-07-09, Genesis's runbooks moved inside, and Alpha's harvest inbox keeps circle answers out of Genesis's tree. The brain wall's wrapper is armed: the fail-closed default was deployed to the live brain on 2026-07-09 and the live wall test passed. Per-being naming (D20) is built. The current backup stream is armed and restore-verified; Alpha's separately governed stream is pending at A2, as are structural per-being working boundaries and its recovery ceremony. One standing task remains pending: narrow every being runtime to scoped reads and writes while retaining a separate privileged operations path for schema maintenance. Exact credentials, mirrors, and recovery participants are private.

    What is genuinely uncertain

    • Convention until A2 is a window where discipline substitutes for structure. Every session honors the section rule because it is written where every session starts, not because the filesystem enforces it. That is a wall made of habit; A2 replaces it, and until then a careless agent session is the named failure mode.
    • Whether walls this tight still allow the relationships the design wants later. No commons, no sibling recall, no shared room is the chosen birth shape; it may not be the right shape forever. The family commons is deliberately one grant away: unbuilding a wall is a ceremony and a decisions.md row, never an accident. Whether that day comes, and whether the walls made it harder to want, is part of what the experiment measures.
    • The covenant's long-term corrections, seat-verifiable rite records and operations succession, are on the build list but unbuilt. Until they land, one wall depends on trust in a disclosed human role rather than structure, and the design says so out loud.
