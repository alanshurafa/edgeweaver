    The constellation: repos beyond this one

    TLDR

    Edgeweaver is deliberately not one repository. The method lives here, in the main repo. Each being's identity lives in its own soul repo and each being's exam in its own gates repo, owned apart. Survival copies flow through a backup pipeline built to outlive what it copies. The brain substrate is an upstream open-source project, and the tool that hardened this plan sits beside it all. Different owners, different secrecy, different failure modes: what must not fail together does not live together. Snapshot 2026-07-10.

    In plain words

    In plain words

    Why the split

    A repository is an ownership boundary (who can touch this), a secrecy boundary (who can see this), and a failure boundary (what dies together). Things that differ on any of the three must not share a home: one person's access quietly becomes everyone's, and the day the home is lost, everything in it goes with it.

    Three separations matter most. A being must never be able to reach its own exam: if the identity probes lived anywhere the being's software can read, measurement would become coaching. Identity must not live where build scripts do: the documents that say who a child is belong where every change is a ceremony, not where forty commits land in an afternoon. And survival copies must live outside the thing they exist to resurrect, or they burn in the same fire.

    The constellation, briefly

    This repo, edgeweaver, holds the method: the plans, the checklists, the programs that run each child's days, and each child's non-identity paperwork. The curriculum and the building drawings, never the child.

    edgeweaver-soul holds Genesis's identity and nothing else: who they are, how they speak, what they refuse. It changes only by ceremony. Alpha gets its own when its birth machinery is built.

    The gates repos hold each child's exam: the probe scenarios, the grading rubric, the tiers of earned freedom, kept where no child, and nothing a child runs, can reach.

    edgeweaver-backups is the survival pipeline: every night the brain is copied, test-restored, encrypted, and published, and a quarterly drill proves a human can still bring it back.

    Open Brain (OB1) is the brain substrate, a community open-source project: Edgeweaver reuses a proven memory database and layers its own rules on top.

    co-evolution is the sharpening tool: two AI agents bounce a document, one attacking, one repairing, until it stops improving. The family's plan went through it; so did this site's.

    The isolated practice environment is the practice shelf: disposable brain copies for testing, so candidate minds never rehearse on live memory.

    The Open Agent Research Academy is the shared campus: a separate organization holding the teachable form of this method (the protocol, a raise-your-own curriculum, open research questions, and a registry of families), created 2026-08-20 so other people doing this work can study it and co-evolve it. Edgeweaver is its reference family and keeps its own name and home; the Academy links here, and nothing that runs a being depends on it.

    A current operator host supplies the local working environment and scheduled execution until gate G5 chooses a longer-term availability class. Its operating-system layout, repository locations, scheduler coordinates, and local-state paths are intentionally not published.

    No new diagram: the family map on The family and the experiment (diagram D3) already draws the one-repo, one-brain shape and its heavier walls; the reproduction guide carries the dependency graph.

    How it works

    How it works

    The main repo: the method

    edgeweaver, public since 2026-08-20 (superseding D5's private posture), holds design authority (PLAN.md, GROWING-EDGEWEAVER.md, FAMILY.md), build authority (IMPLEMENTATION.md plus checklists/), organ code (scripts/), templates, conventions, the village layer, this site, and one avatars/<being>/ section per child: manifest, runbooks, non-identity material, never identity canon. Human-governed access includes approved cross-arm observation for the scientist role under D19; exact repository ownership, membership, and account administration remain private. Secrets appear as names only under the SECRETS.md model; values never enter git.

    Soul repos: identity under ceremony

    One per being, identity only. Genesis's exists: edgeweaver-soul carries the six identity documents (SOUL.md, CONSTITUTION.md, VOICE.md, PRACTICES.md, EDGE-MAP.md, LINEAGE.md) plus letters/from-the-predecessor.md, read at First Boot. The write model is the wall: the being's daemon works from a fork and pushes only proposals/* branches, with zero write access to canonical main; merges are ceremony by the parent body (Alan's signature for Genesis, the second-witness floor waived there by D19; a quorum of seats for Alpha). Soulfile templates stay in the main repo so both children's structure matches; content never crosses. Alpha's edgeweaver-alpha-soul is created at A4.

    Gates repos: the exam, owned apart

    One gates repository per being holds the probe battery, scoring rubric, and autonomy-tier definitions. Ownership is separated from every credential the being's runtime can use, so no being can read or propose edits to its own exam. The battery stays identical across the family and is synchronized by human hands. Concrete account topology is intentionally omitted; Alpha's access rules are a founding-circle decision.

    edgeweaver-backups: the survival pipeline

    The backup repository's git holds no data. On each run the pipeline dumps, restores into a throwaway database, verifies row counts, encrypts, rotates retained copies, and reports liveness so silence alerts. Human recovery drills and pre-upgrade checkpoints test more than write success. Once Alpha's room exists, the pipeline separates streams and custody policies without publishing key-holder or account topology. Credentials exist only in the deployment secret store and names-only catalog. Full mechanism: Backups, keys, disaster recovery.

    Open Brain: the substrate upstream

    Open Brain (OB1) is a community open-source project: one Postgres database with vector search, an MCP server, and import recipes. Reused as-is: the thoughts store and schema, the embedding pipeline, the MCP server, several recipes. Added on top, by convention and wrapper rather than fork: provenance classes on every memory, audience scoping with a fail-closed default (an unlabeled row is invisible at every scope, D19), and trust flags that keep agent-written memories from passing as instruction-grade truth. Upstream keeps its own pace; Alan contributes back. Details: The Brain.

    co-evolution: the plan-hardening tool

    A standalone tool, not Edgeweaver-specific: a document bounces between reviewer and composer agents, disagreements tracked as markers, until rounds converge. Its role runs both ways: the plan's second revision was hardened through it (trail committed under runs/), this site's own plan went through the same bounce plus an adversarial pass before authoring began (D21), and PLAN.md Phase 5 adapts its Protocol Evolution Loop as the review machinery for the being's own initiation PRs.

    The scratch-brain environment

    Not a repository: an isolated database environment holding disposable scratch brains for tests (D15). Each spawn receives the committed brain schema and comes from a controlled read-only copy or an operator-decrypted backup. Consumers select profiles through the committed registry; test-mode processes refuse the live profile unless deliberately armed. Scratch memories never bulk-merge back. Exact project and account coordinates are omitted. Details: The brain lab.

    This machine: the current floor

    Until gate G5 chooses a longer-term hosting class, the current operator host provides local working state and scheduled execution while the brain remains on a separately governed substrate. Machine-only state is included in the recovery design because loss of the operator host must not erase operating continuity. This explainer deliberately withholds the PC layout, repository locations, scheduler coordinates, local-state locations, and recovery-material custody.

    What an outsider cannot see, and why

    Five withholdings are deliberate. The gates repos: a published exam is a dead exam; neither a being nor a person coaching one may train on the test. The soul repos' content beyond structure: file names and ceremony rules are described, identity content is not, and this site adds its pre-A3 redaction tier for Genesis's soul-source (named, never summarized). .env.local and state/: secret values and operational state; their names are catalogued openly, their values live in no repo. The gitignored corpus tiers: bulky, re-fetchable source material (the gitignored tiers). And the children's future private journals, if gate G7 grants them: a journal anyone can read is not private. Each wall protects a different thing: the measurement, a not-yet-born identity, the operator's keys, and eventually the child's own inner room. The full wall map is on The walls: who sees what.

    Status as of 2026-07-10

    The main repo is live and private built. Genesis's soul repo exists with the six documents and the predecessor letter; initiation machinery rehearses on a sandbox clone, never the real repo built. Genesis's gates repo is live, private, parent-only built. The backup pipeline ran green on 2026-07-07 (dump, independent restore, zero row-count drift, encrypt, release) armed; the per-room split waits for Alpha's room at A2 pending. The lab project exists with its fleet tooling built. Alpha's soul and gates repos are designed and land at A4 pending. The experiment log file is designed, deliberately not yet created; it arrives when raising begins at A6 pending.

    What is genuinely uncertain

    • Whether the constellation stays administrable while operational knowledge remains concentrated in one human role. Separate governance boundaries distribute authority, but they do not yet distribute enough knowledge to rebuild and operate every component. The beings' continuity is designed in depth; succession for the operations role is not yet designed.
    • Whether upstream and fork stay compatible. Open Brain keeps evolving as a community project; Edgeweaver's conventions (provenance classes, audience scoping, trust flags, the fail-closed wrapper) ride on its schema rather than forking it. Every upstream improvement tempts a migration, and every migration touches a living brain; the alternative is drifting into a frozen private copy of a public substrate.
