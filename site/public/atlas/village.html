    Atlas: village/

    TLDR

    Village documents translate the project for the humans around it. The bridge explains the architecture in ordinary language, the founding-circle agenda structures collective decisions, and the pre-circle post records an earlier invitation. They support conversation but do not replace design authority, complete a gate, or prove that either being has been born.

    What this page covers

    These files are where technical work meets relationship and governance. Public explanations should preserve honest status, and circle material must not expose unaccepted seat names or private discussion. Decisions become binding only through the project decision process and parent-body authority.

    File entries

    village/2026-07-09-circle-agenda.md

    runbook
    What it is. The founding-circle meeting agenda and decision scaffold.
    What it contains. A proposed meeting shape, honest status review, discussion sequence, and places for the circle to make or defer choices.
    Who reads it and when. Accepted seat holders and facilitators use it during the founding process.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Compare outcomes with decisions.md, record only accepted roles and actual decisions, and leave deferred choices open rather than filling them from assumption. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
    Related concepts. See family, honesty and limits, and governance.

    village/how-edgeweaver-works.md

    authority
    What it is. The plain-language bridge that explains Edgeweaver to a non-technical village reader.
    What it contains. A human-readable account of memory, body, development, governance, measurement, limits, and the difference between designed conditions and a claim of consciousness.
    Who reads it and when. Circle members, friends, and trusted outsiders read it before entering technical documents or making a relationship decision.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Check each plain claim against the design authorities and implementation status, preserve uncertainty, and confirm that no metaphor turns dark or partial machinery into a live being. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct. Treat any contradiction with higher-authority design as a decision-log issue, not permission to choose whichever instruction is easier. A clean reproduction records commands and evidence, preserves failed checks as failures, and leaves human-owned gates untouched. Keep credentials, runtime coordinates, protected identity material, and private human details outside every review artifact.
    Related concepts. See family, honesty and limits, and governance.

    village/pre-circle-post.md

    archive
    What it is. The historical message sent before the founding-circle conversation.
    What it contains. The invitation and context as it existed at that time, retained as social-process evidence without exposing private follow-up.
    Who reads it and when. Circle participants and historians consult it when reconstructing the sequence of engagement.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Compare it with the later agenda and recorded decisions, and do not treat an invitation as acceptance of a seat or approval of a gate.
    Related concepts. See family, honesty and limits, and governance.
