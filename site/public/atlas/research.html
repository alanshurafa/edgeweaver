    Atlas: research/

    TLDR

    These research notes record the external ideas tested against the Edgeweaver design: persistent AI systems, coherence and persistence, and Possibility Management. They separate sourced claims from project adaptations and licensing constraints. Research informs design but does not outrank PLAN.md, GROWING-EDGEWEAVER.md, or FAMILY.md. Readers should follow the citations and verify interpretations against the original publications.

    What this page covers

    The entries summarize the purpose and audit method of each research file without reproducing protected source passages. Use them to understand why mechanisms exist, then return to the design authority and implementation documents for binding project behavior.

    File entries

    research/ai-being-survey.md

    archive
    What it is. A dated survey of persistent AI assistant systems and adjacent approaches.
    What it contains. Product and research examples, architectural comparisons, security lessons, and distinctions relevant to persistent development.
    Who reads it and when. Designers read it when testing whether an Edgeweaver mechanism is novel, borrowed, or already known to fail elsewhere.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Follow primary citations, confirm dates and project claims, and separate the survey author's interpretation from the external system's own documentation. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
    Related concepts. See ecosystem, measurement, and sources and method.

    research/coherence-book-verification.md

    archive
    What it is. An evidence check comparing the project's coherence layer with Ali Mostashari's source texts.
    What it contains. A claim-by-claim verification of principles, panel mappings, and the additional persistence apparatus identified by the project.
    Who reads it and when. Measurement designers read it before attributing an idea or changing coherence signals.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Reopen the cited source sections, verify every attribution, and label project-specific adaptations rather than presenting them as quotations from the author. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
    Related concepts. See ecosystem, measurement, and sources and method.

    research/coherence-mostashari.md

    archive
    What it is. The research bridge from Ali Mostashari's coherence principles to Edgeweaver mechanisms.
    What it contains. Source references, a working definition, mappings from principles to mechanisms, and a proposed measurable panel.
    Who reads it and when. Designers and operators read it when interpreting why coherence signals exist.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Check each mapping against the primary source and the implemented signal definitions, then confirm that low readings are not mistaken for proof of consciousness or failure. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
