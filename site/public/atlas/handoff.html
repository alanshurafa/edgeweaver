    Atlas: handoff/

    TLDR

    Handoff documents preserve enough context for another operator or agent to continue a complicated build or test arc without guessing. One explains the autonomous dark-build loop; the other records the voice testing architecture and current findings. They are operational guides and historical evidence, not authority to arm disabled components or publish deployment coordinates.

    What this page covers

    Read a handoff after the higher-authority design and implementation texts. Confirm its status claims against the current ledger and code because handoffs describe a point in time. Sensitive values and machine targeting details remain outside the tracked document.

    File entries

    handoff/prebuild-loop.md

    archive
    What it is. A historical handoff recording the autonomous dark-build loop that completed the pre-birth backend track.
    What it contains. The former provisioning boundaries, iteration body, verification and failure handling, and disabled-by-default rule, preserved as evidence of how the loop ran.
    Who reads it and when. Auditors consult it to reconstruct the completed PREBUILD run. Current operators do not resume it; they start with START-HERE.md, the current IMPLEMENTATION.md ledger, and the matching checklist.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Compare its recorded outcomes with PREBUILD.md and the ops-log final report, then follow the current ledger and checklist for any remaining or new work. Treat superseded provisioning as history, never an active instruction.
    Related concepts. See dark build, voice, and operations.

    handoff/voice-testing-notes.md

    archive
    What it is. The continuity record for the realtime voice testing arc.
    What it contains. The current test architecture, known findings, invocation shape, versioned state, and unresolved quality questions without live network coordinates.
    Who reads it and when. A voice engineer reads it before reproducing tests or interpreting earlier results.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Compare notes with current code and test fixtures, run the documented local checks, and treat historical findings as evidence rather than present-tense guarantees. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
    Related concepts. See dark build, voice, and operations.
