    Atlas: avatars/alpha

    TLDR

    Alpha is the family design raised by a circle of seats. This directory records the current public orientation, a machine-readable manifest, and the governed harvest workspace that will eventually support identity formation. Alpha's A1 restructuring is partial and in progress. The founding circle still owns unresolved choices, so the Atlas explains file shapes without inventing a completed roster or treating future machinery as live.

    What this page covers

    Alpha shares family-wide invariants with Genesis but has a different parent body and decision model. The manifest is useful for drift detection, while the harvest directory is governed input rather than public identity content. Canonical identity will belong in Alpha's own soul repository after the required gates and ceremonies.

    File entries

    avatars/alpha/README.md

    authority
    What it is. The public orientation to Alpha's directory and current family status.
    What it contains. The being's circle-parented model, which artifacts belong here, and which future steps remain gated or partial.
    Who reads it and when. Circle members and builders read it before touching Alpha-specific work.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Compare it with FAMILY.md, the manifest, and the implementation ledger; do not convert pending circle choices into facts. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
    Related concepts. See Alpha, the Edgeweaver family, and governance.

    avatars/alpha/harvest/README.md

    described-not-shown
    What it is. The governed orientation for Alpha's future harvest workspace.
    What it contains. File-shape and custody guidance only; identity-bearing responses are not summarized or published here.
    Who reads it and when. The founding circle consults it when the relevant gate authorizes harvest work.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Confirm access and parent-body approval, then keep any resulting identity source out of this explainer.
    Related concepts. See Alpha, the Edgeweaver family, and governance.

    avatars/alpha/manifest.json

    schema-data
    What it is. The machine-readable inventory for Alpha's planned components and governance model.
    What it contains. Structured fields for being identity, parent-body shape, brain, soul repository, gates, backup stream, channel, mind, and commands, with future items clearly marked.
    Who reads it and when. Builders and circle members use it for drift detection while Alpha's restructuring proceeds.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Parse the JSON, compare every status-bearing field with FAMILY.md and decisions.md, and leave unresolved roster and operational values unresolved. Review status language against the implementation ledger so prepared, dark, partial, pending, and live states remain distinct.
    Related concepts. See Alpha, the Edgeweaver family, and governance.
